02 / Selected work

Research & projects

01

RL4F · Offline RL for Nuclear Fusion

An open-source benchmark for tokamak plasma control, bringing together environments, datasets, 12 baseline algorithms, and evaluation across 6 profile-tracking tasks.

My contributions include data pipelines, recurrent dynamics modeling, algorithm benchmarking, and multi-GPU hyperparameter tuning.

02

TriShield · Image Copyright Protection

A three-layer protection framework combining external watermarking, intrinsic watermarking, and feature hashing for tamper localization. Built augmentation and attack-simulation pipelines for large-scale image evaluation.
